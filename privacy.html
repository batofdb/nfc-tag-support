Privacy Policy

NFC Tag, by NT Labs. Last updated 20 August 2026.

The short version: the app doesn't collect anything, doesn't send anything anywhere, and doesn't know who you are. What follows is the same thing said carefully.

What we collect

Nothing. There's no account to create, no sign-in, no email address to hand over, and no analytics or tracking of any kind. The app has no advertising and no third-party SDKs.

What stays on your device

The app saves the tags you choose to keep, your recent copies, and any hex commands you've saved. All of it is stored locally on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. It never leaves the device unless you explicitly export it.

Exporting produces a file that you control. Where it goes - Files, Mail, AirDrop, somewhere else - is entirely your choice, and the app isn't involved after you've chosen.

Deleting the app removes everything it stored. There's no copy anywhere else, which also means there's no way to recover it, so export anything you'd miss.

Network access

The app makes no network requests. It has no server, and there is no backend to send anything to. If you tap a link that a tag contains, that opens in your browser and is between you and that website.

NFC data

Reading a tag gives the app that tag's contents and identifiers. That information is used to show you what's on the tag and, if you ask it to, to save or copy that tag. It isn't transmitted anywhere.

Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date at the top. Since the app collects nothing, any change is likely to be a clarification rather than a change in what happens to your data.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or anything else, can go here: github.com/batofdb/nfc-tag-support/issues
